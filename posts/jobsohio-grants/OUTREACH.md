# Outreach: Every JobsOhio grant, in one searchable tool

## 4a. The list

Fourteen people and newsrooms. Picked for recent work on JobsOhio, on Ohio
economic development incentives, or on a place this project names. No party
organizations or campaigns.

**On contacts.** Verified means the address is published on the
organization's own page and I read it there. Several Ohio newsrooms block
automated requests, so where no individual address is public I say so and
name the published desk address instead. Nothing here is guessed.

**On links.** Ohio Capital Journal and Cleveland.com return 403 to automated
fetches, so the URLs for those pieces are built from each site's own URL
pattern and could not be opened to confirm. Headline, outlet and date are
exact, taken from the outlets' own feeds. The two marked **verified link**
were opened and read.
